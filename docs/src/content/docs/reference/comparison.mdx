MikroDocs is a local-first Markdown writing app for owned documents, exports, and browser storage. It is not trying to replace collaborative office suites.

It is not meant to be better than every alternative. It is meant to be a better fit when ownership, simplicity, and a smaller operational surface matter.

## Quick Comparison

| Area | MikroDocs | Common alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Format | Markdown-first documents | Rich docs, databases, notes, or proprietary formats |
| Storage | Browser-owned and portable | Cloud workspaces, sync vaults, or local files |
| Collaboration | Simple and optional where available | Often the main product surface |
| Exports | Built around keeping documents portable | Varies by platform |
| Best fit | Quiet writing and owned Markdown | Real-time editing or large knowledge bases |

## Where MikroDocs Fits

- You want a quiet writing tool with portable output.
- You prefer local-first document ownership.
- You use Markdown as a durable format.

## When Another Tool May Be Better

- You need multi-user editing, comments, and approvals.
- You need database-backed knowledge management.
- You rely on office-suite formatting and review workflows.

## Bottom Line

Choose MikroDocs when you want a focused tool you can understand, run, and keep. Choose a larger platform when you need its scale, ecosystem, managed operations, or specialized workflows.
